The routing USP

Optimise for arrival, not just a coloured line.

BulletROUTE is designed to compare viable journey alternatives and surface the earliest checked arrival among the routes it evaluates, with route state clearly shown rather than hidden behind a generic ETA.

01

Exact vehicle profile

Plan using the vehicle dimensions and weight that actually matter to the journey.

02

Depart At / Arrive By

Plan forward from departure or work backwards to the latest feasible departure for a required arrival.

03

Road-state aware

Evaluate the journey against known restrictions, closures and changing traffic conditions without exposing raw feeds to the user.

04

Multi-stop missions

Required stops and service time stay part of the journey instead of being treated as an afterthought.

05

Decision status

Checked, review and rejected states make route confidence visible to the planner.

06

Live recheck

Re-evaluate when conditions move, then compare the new outcome before changing the operational plan.
