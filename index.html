One product family

A closed operating loop.

BulletROUTE makes the route decision. BulletFLEET turns it into an operational plan and keeps the Control Tower informed. BulletNAV executes it with the driver and feeds live progress back into the stack.

01

Decide

Plan against the exact vehicle, timing requirement, required stops and current road conditions.

02

Control

Assign people, vehicles, routes and shifts while keeping live journey state visible to operations.

03

Execute

Give the driver a clear, route-focused interface with explicit recheck and route-change decisions.
